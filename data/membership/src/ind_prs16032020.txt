                                     PRESS RELEASE
Mumbai, March 16, 2020

                                 Replacements in Indices

Yes Bank removed from all NIFTY equity indices; Sterling and Wilson Solar brought forward as its NIFTY 500 replacement.
The changes shall become effective from March 19, 2020 (close of March 18, 2020).

1)      NIFTY 500

The following companies are being excluded:

     Sr. No.                 Company Name                                   Symbol
       1      Yes Bank Ltd.                                      YESBANK

The following companies are being included:

     Sr. No.                 Company Name                                   Symbol
       1      Sterling And Wilson Solar Ltd.                     SWSOLAR
