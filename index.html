    What the rules have earned walk-forward backtest, 2025-01 → 2026-10, costs included

    Momentum engine (test window May 2025 → Oct 2026, a falling market): +0.7% against the Nifty's −7.1%, max drawdown 14%, 64 trades, 34% winners. Across a 96-cell parameter sweep 83 cells beat the index over the same window and 47 made money outright. One falling year says it protected capital; it says nothing yet about how it compounds in a rising one. Full reports: reports/momentum-*.md.
    Swing engine: over 157 trades the combined rules returned −0.06R per trade (profit factor 0.87, 43% win rate, max drawdown 23R). Each setup alone is slightly positive at a 2R target (pullback +0.11R over 146 trades, breakout +0.05R over 123) and the difference between configurations is inside the noise of a two-year sample. Treat every row above as a hypothesis with roughly even odds and a small positive edge at best; the score (0–100) has not separated winners from losers so far. Full tables, parameter sweep and every trade: reports/ in the repository. Run node scripts/backtest.mjs after any rule change.
