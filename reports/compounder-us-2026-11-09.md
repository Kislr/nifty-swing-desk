## How to read this

Every number is as first reported in the 10-K that covered that year (point-in-time), not as later restated. ROCE = operating income / (total assets − current liabilities); the rule is 4 of 5 years ≥ 12% and no year under 8%. Gross margin must be within 3 points of its 5-year high. Revenue CAGR ≥ 10% with diluted shares up ≤ 10%. 3-year OCF / net income ≥ 0.8. Net debt / EBITDA ≤ 2 (net cash passes). Rank = sum of the five per-trait ranks, no weights. None of this has been backtested yet; see SCREEN.md for the pass criteria it must meet.
