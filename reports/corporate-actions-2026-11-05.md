## How to read this

Factor = what the pre-event price is divided by (a 1:1 bonus is 2; a ₹10 → ₹2 split is 5; a consolidation is below 1). Observed = close before the ex-date ÷ open on it; an event is applied when observed is within 12% of the factor. A rejected event usually means NSE's ex-date differs from the bar where the gap is, or the line was mis-read — check the `checked against` column and, if the factor is right, set `apply: true` by hand in data/adjustments.json. engine.buildBars applies the table at load; the history files stay raw.
